# Story 02: Do Bhikshu Aur Behti Nadi (दो भिक्षु और बहती नदी)
## Theme: Overthinking & Letting Go of the Past

### Format & Aesthetic Specifications
* **Target Duration**: ~45–50 seconds (1350–1500 frames @ 30 fps)
* **Resolution**: 1080×1920 (9:16 Vertical Reel)
* **Visual Style**: Traditional Warli Tribal Art (Maharashtra):
  - **Background**: Terracotta earthen mud-wall (`#8D3929` / `#7A2E20` with organic textured grain).
  - **Color Palette**: Off-white rice-paste (`#FBF8EE`, `#FFFDF7`) on terracotta, with subtle ochre accents (`#E6A742`) for highlights.
  - **Figures**: The authentic Warli way:
    - Circle head with hair bun / topknot.
    - Two inverted triangles meeting at the tip forming the torso and hips.
    - Linear arms and legs with rhythmic bends and gestures.
    - Monks with simple shaved head / halo dot; young woman with traditional hair bun; elder monk with walking staff.
  - **Environment**: Warli spiral foliage trees with perched birds, thatched triangular mud huts, dotted trails (*tarpa* path rhythm), a sun with geometric sawtooth rays, undulating linear river waves, and a complete perimeter border of small repeating triangles.
  - **Captions**: Dynamic 3–4 word chunking in safe zone, spoken word highlighted in bright ochre-gold (`#FBC02D`).

### 45-Second Hinglish Voice-Over Script (7 Beats)

**Scene 1 (0s – 6s) — The Question (सवाल)**
> *"अगर चोट सिर्फ एक बार लगी थी, तो उसका दर्द मन में सौ बार क्यों दोहराते हो?"*
> *(If the hurt happened only once, why replay the pain a hundred times in your mind?)*

**Scene 2 (6s – 12s) — The Setting (पृष्ठभूमि)**
> *"दो भिक्षु जंगल के रास्ते से अपने आश्रम लौट रहे थे। सामने एक उफनती हुई कीचड़ भरी नदी थी।"*
> *(Two monks were walking through the forest back to their ashram. Ahead lay a swollen, muddy river.)*

**Scene 3 (12s – 18s) — The Encounter (मुलाकात)**
> *"किनारे पर एक युवती खड़ी थी, जो उस तेज़ बहाव को पार करने से डर रही थी।"*
> *(On the bank stood a young woman, terrified of crossing the torrential current.)*

**Scene 4 (18s – 25s) — The Action (कर्म)**
> *"बिना कुछ कहे, बड़े भिक्षु ने उसे अपनी पीठ पर उठाया . नदी पार कराई, और नीचे उतारकर आगे बढ़ गए।"*
> *(Without a word, the elder monk lifted her onto his back . carried her across the river, set her down, and walked on.)*

**Scene 5 (25s – 32s) — The Turmoil (भीतरी द्वंद्व)**
> *"घंटों तक दोनों चुपचाप चलते रहे। पर छोटे भिक्षु का मन गुस्से और बेचैनी से उबल रहा था।"*
> *(For hours, both walked in silence. But the younger monk's mind was boiling with restless frustration.)*

**Scene 6 (32s – 40s) — The Questioning (आरोप)**
> *"आखिरकार उसने चिल्लाकर कहा—'हमारे नियमों में स्त्री को छूना मना है, फिर आपने उसे पीठ पर क्यों उठाया?'"*
> *(Finally, he burst out—'Our sacred vows forbid touching a woman, why did you carry her on your back?')*

**Scene 7 (40s – 48s) — The Answer & Takeaway (बोध)**
> *"बड़े भिक्षु मुस्कुराए और बोले—'वत्स, मैंने तो उसे नदी किनारे ही छोड़ दिया था। तुम अब तक उसे अपने सिर पर क्यों उठाए घूम रहे हो?'"*
> *(The elder monk smiled and replied—'Child, I left her by the river bank hours ago. Why are you still carrying her in your head?')*
