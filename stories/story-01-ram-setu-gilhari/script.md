# Story 01: Ram Setu Aur Nanhi Gilhari (राम सेतु और नन्ही गिलहरी)

## Format & Style Specifications
* **Target Duration**: ~45 seconds (1350 frames @ 30 fps)
* **Resolution**: 1080×1920 (9:16 Vertical Reel)
* **Visual Style**: Traditional Madhubani (Mithila) Folk Painting:
  - Double black ink outlines (`#1A1A1A`).
  - Flat pigment fills: Madhubani Red (`#D32F2F`), Turmeric Yellow (`#FBC02D`), Leaf Green (`#2E7D32`), Indigo Blue (`#1565C0`), Natural Cream (`#FFF8E7`).
  - Traditional Kachni fine-line hatching patterns on attire and nature.
  - Almond-shaped eyes, Madhubani Sun with serene human face, sacred Tree of Life with birds, patterned fish in the waves, geometric red/black border.
  - Strictly flat, zero diffuse shading.
* **Deity Portrayal**: Bhagwan Shri Ram drawn with serene, respectful, compassionate facial expressions and traditional iconography (pitambara, neel-varna, crown with peacock feather).
* **Captions**: 3 to 4 words at a time, active word highlighted in bright turmeric yellow, strictly inside the Instagram safe zone (top > 160px, bottom > 340px).

---

## 45-Second Hinglish Voice-Over Script

**Scene 1 (0s – 6s) — The Question**
> *"Kya ek mutthi-bhar gilahri vishaal samundar par pathar ka pul bana sakti hai?"*

**Scene 2 (6s – 12s) — Setting**
> *"Rameshwaram ka kinara tha, jahan vanar sena vishaal parvat utha kar samundar mein daal rahi thi."*

**Scene 3 (12s – 18s) — Problem**
> *"Har taraf dhool thi, garjana thi, aur Lanka tak pahunchne ki bechaini thi."*

**Scene 4 (18s – 25s) — The Turn**
> *"Tabhi ek nanhi gilahri paani mein bheegti, ret par lot ti . aur apne shareer se do-do daane pul par jhaad aati."*

**Scene 5 (25s – 31s) — Choice / The Scorn**
> *"Ek balwaan vanar ne gusse mein kaha—'Aye chhoti! Hat raste se, tere do daanon se kya Ram Setu banega?'"*

**Scene 6 (31s – 39s) — The Answer**
> *"Tabhi Shri Ram ne muskurakar use apni hatheli par utha liya, aur bole—'Seva mein shakti nahi, shraddha dekhi jaati hai.'"*

**Scene 7 (39s – 45s) — Next Part & The Blessing**
> *"Ram ji ne prem se uski peeth par ungliyan pheri, aur teen divya lakirein ban gayi. Agli baar jab tum gilahri dekho, to dekhna—kya wo lakirein tumhe dikhti hain?"*
